Prompts for a truthful STAR answer
  • Situation: What does the listener need to understand the problem?
  • Task: What were you responsible for, and what belonged to someone else?
  • Action: What did you actually do? Explain one useful decision.
  • Result: What did you observe? Keep estimates, reported feedback and unknowns distinct.

You do not need a success story for every question. Study, volunteering and unfinished work can provide real examples.
